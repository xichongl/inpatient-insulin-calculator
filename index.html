⚕️ Clinical Decision Support Tool

For licensed healthcare professionals only. This tool provides decision support for inpatient subcutaneous insulin dosing based on published Endotext / NCBI guidelines (NBK278972, Endocrine Society 2012 guidelines, ADA 2019 Inpatient Standards).

Recommendations are suggestions only and must be reviewed and approved by the responsible clinician. Individual patient factors, institutional protocols, pharmacist input, and clinical judgment always take precedence.

This tool does not replace formal diabetes consultation. Patients with complex insulin requirements, hypoglycemia unawareness, DKA, or HHS should have endocrinology involvement.
